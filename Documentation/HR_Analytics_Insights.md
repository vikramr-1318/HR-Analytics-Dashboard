# 👥 HR Analytics & Employee Turnover — Analytical Insights & Findings

## Executive Summary
Analysis of 1,470 employees from the IBM HR Analytics dataset reveals a **16.12% overall attrition rate** (237 departed employees). The data demonstrates that employee turnover is not evenly distributed; it is heavily concentrated among specific roles, compensation tiers, and workload patterns.

---

## 1. Primary Drivers of Employee Turnover

### A. Overtime Burnout: The Single Strongest Predictor
* **Employees Working Overtime:**
  * Total Headcount: 416 employees
  * Attrition Count: 127 employees
  * **Attrition Rate: 30.53%**
* **Employees NOT Working Overtime:**
  * Total Headcount: 1,054 employees
  * Attrition Count: 110 employees
  * **Attrition Rate: 10.44%**
* **Finding:** Employees required to work overtime are **2.92x more likely to leave** than those working standard hours.

### B. High-Risk Job Roles
* **Sales Representatives:** Highest attrition at **39.76%** (33 out of 83 departed). Driven by high quota pressure and low base compensation ($2,626/mo avg).
* **Laboratory Technicians:** **23.94%** attrition (62 out of 259 departed).
* **Human Resources Personnel:** **23.08%** attrition (12 out of 52 departed).
* **Low-Risk Roles:** Research Directors (2.50%) and Managers (4.90%) exhibit superior retention (>95%).

### C. Compensation & Salary Band Thresholds
* **Low Salary Band (<=$5,000 / mo USD):**
  * 749 employees | **21.76% attrition**
* **Medium Salary Band ($5,001 - $10,000 / mo USD):**
  * 440 employees | **10.23% attrition**
* **High Salary Band (>$10,000 / mo USD):**
  * 281 employees | **9.61% attrition**
* **Finding:** Turnover drops by **more than half** once an employee advances past the $5,000/month threshold.

### D. Age & Career Stage Vulnerability
* **Young Professionals (Age 18-25):** Attrition exceeds **35.8%**, reflecting early-career mobility and job-hopping.
* **Core Career (Age 31-45):** Stabilizes at **11.4% - 13.5%**.
* **Senior Staff (Age 55+):** 15.6% attrition (transition to retirement or consulting).

---

## 2. Retention Impact of Work-Life Balance and Satisfaction
* Employees reporting **Low Job Satisfaction (Rating = 1)** experience **22.8% attrition** compared to only **11.3%** for High Satisfaction (Rating = 4).
* Employees with **Bad Work-Life Balance (Rating = 1)** have an attrition rate of **31.25%**, confirming that personal flexibility directly governs employee loyalty.

---

## 3. Financial Impact & Currency Clarification
* **Dataset Currency Standard:** Compensation values in this dataset represent **United States Dollars ($ USD)**. The average monthly income is **$6,503 USD** ($78,036/year).
* **Cost of Turnover:** Replacing an employee costs an estimated 50% to 150% of annual salary. At an average salary of $78K, the 237 departures represent an estimated organizational replacement and onboarding cost of **$9.2M - $18.5M**.

---

## 4. Actionable Retention Strategy
1. **Overtime Cap & Compensation Review:** Implement a strict overtime approval threshold and transition critical night/weekend workloads to temporary contractor support.
2. **Sales Representative Career Ladder:** Restructure commission plans and career milestones for Sales Representatives to mitigate early departure.
3. **First-Year Mentorship:** Establish a structured 90-day onboarding journey for new hires under age 30 to curb early attrition.
